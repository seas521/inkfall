## 实际生成提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent layered scenery asset for a monochrome 2D side-scrolling atmospheric forest game. Generate one wide 1536x1024 RGBA asset of submerged natural riverbank geology ONLY, no water, no scenery backdrop, genuinely transparent negative space. This is NOT a finished scene or a diagram. A left bank with organically eroded soil, a few irregular rounded embedded river boulders and one fine worn root descends steeply from the left edge at 25% image height into the lower center; a much smaller unrelated right bank rises toward the lower right edge. The central and upper 65% of the canvas is transparent open space. Composition MUST be strongly asymmetric, a few large quiet masses, not a continuous horizontal floor, no evenly spaced stone row, no repeating pebble pattern. Stones are partially embedded, have convincing organic volume and weathered natural silhouettes, overlapping masses show depth without any drawn outlines. Art direction: LIMBO-like black and white cinematic photographic silhouettes, dark charcoal and near-black soft masses with restrained low-key broad grayscale lighting on just a few planes, no high-frequency texture, slightly defocused edges, no grey/white outline or halo. The slope is seen from a strict side-on camera with slight depth recession, not a top-down texture, not isometric, not an illustrative sticker, not a cartoon rock pile. No hard polygonal facets, no uniformly smooth blobs, no noise filter, no glowing caustics, no waterline, no reflections, no mountains, no trees, no sky, no text. Let the lower and outer masses continue beyond the canvas edges. Preserve real alpha; transparent regions must remain empty.
